What type of services do you provide?

I provide technical and consulting solutions including infrastructure, networking, cybersecurity, cloud computing, virtualization, backup, access management, technical documentation, and troubleshooting.

Are the services for individuals or companies?

The services are suitable for companies, organizations, small and medium offices, and individuals who need structured technical support or consultation.

Do you provide remote support or on-site visits?

Some services can be delivered remotely, while others may require an on-site visit depending on the request and scope of work.

How can I request a service?

You can submit a request through the service request form on the website, select the service type, and provide the basic details for review.

Is the cost fixed?

The cost is determined after reviewing the scope of work, service type, complexity, and expected time required.

Do you provide a report or recommendations after the service?

Yes, a technical summary or practical recommendations can be provided depending on the service type and agreed scope.

Can I request consultation only?

Yes, technical consultation can be requested to assess the current situation or suggest suitable solutions before implementation.

Do the services include cybersecurity?

Yes, services may include reviewing basic protection, improving device security, organizing permissions, and reducing risks.

Do the services include cloud computing and virtualization?

Yes, services may include support and organization of cloud and virtualization environments, resource management, performance improvement, and backup.

Is client data handled confidentially?

Yes, any shared information or data is handled confidentially and only within the scope of the requested service.
